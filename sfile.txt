SIMPLE SUMMARY

PHASE 1:
Give it startup info -> it reads a due-diligence checklist webpage, searches
it for relevant parts, and asks an AI to write the Top 10 smart questions
an investor would ask. Prints them in the terminal.

PHASE 2:
Turns those Top 10 questions into a live Q&A. You answer one question at a
time. After each answer, the AI checks if it needs to ask a follow-up
question based on what you said. If yes, it asks that. If no, it moves to
the next Top 10 question. Runs as a small web server, saves everything in
a local database.
